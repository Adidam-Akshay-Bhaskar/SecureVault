Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SecureVault.

SecureVault is a secure, end-to-end encrypted cloud storage platform designed to protect your most sensitive digital assets — documents, passwords, keys, and files — using military-grade AES-256 encryption and a zero-knowledge architecture.
When you upload a file or save data, it is encrypted on your device before being transmitted to our servers. Only you hold the decryption key — derived from your master password. Even SecureVault staff cannot read your data.
Each account includes 2.5 GB of secure encrypted storage. Storage is measured after encryption, so the actual usable size is equivalent to your raw file sizes.
Files can be shared directly within the app or via secure links. You can share with other SecureVault users in-app, or generate a secure link to send to anyone — no account required on their end.
Yes. All data is encrypted client-side with AES-256 before reaching our servers. Even in the event of a server breach, attackers would only find encrypted blobs — completely unreadable without your master password.
SecureVault uses AES-256-GCM for file encryption, PBKDF2 with SHA-256 (100,000 iterations) for key derivation from your master password, and TLS 1.3 for all data in transit. This is the same standard used by banks and government agencies.
Yes. You can log in to SecureVault from any modern browser on any device — desktop, laptop, tablet, or mobile. Your encrypted vault syncs automatically across all sessions using secure token-based authentication.
You can initiate account recovery using your registered email. A secure recovery link will be sent, allowing you to reset your master password or Security PIN. Note: due to zero-knowledge design, previously encrypted files may not be recoverable if the key changes.
SecureVault supports all file types — PDFs, images (JPG, PNG, HEIC), documents (DOCX, XLSX, PPTX), ZIP archives, videos, audio files, and more. There is no restriction on file format; only storage limits apply.
Yes. Every account includes 2.5 GB of secure encrypted storage and full end-to-end encryption — no credit card required. There are currently no premium plans.
Zero-knowledge means SecureVault's servers never have access to your plaintext data or encryption keys. Your master password is never transmitted — it is used locally to derive an encryption key, which encrypts your data before it leaves your browser.
You can request account deletion from the Settings section inside your vault dashboard. Once confirmed, all your encrypted data is permanently wiped from our servers within 72 hours. This action is irreversible.
